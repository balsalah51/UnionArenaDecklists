Rimuru strategy

Blue Rimuru in That Time I Got Reincarnated As A Slime is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 2. This site is hosting 234 public 50-card lists, including 22 recent top 8s. The tier list has Rimuru in B.

How the raid face works

UE20BT/TSK-1-107 · cost 4 · 4000 BP · Blue

The printed trigger on Rimuru (UE20BT/TSK-1-107) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Rimuru (UE20BT/TSK-1-107) and Soei (UE20BT/TSK-1-060). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that the dense Rimuru raid package (017 into 107, 053, 052, 050) lets the deck consistently evolve into powerful late-game bodies while thinning redundant copies from the deck. The same snapshot flags that against explosive tempo decks like Evangelion - Rei Ayanami or Evangelion - Evangelion Test Type-01 Pseudo DMS Phase, Rimuru's build-up plan can fall behind before its raids come online.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Rimuru, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Feast (062) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.
