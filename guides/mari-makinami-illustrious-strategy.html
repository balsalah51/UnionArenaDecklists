Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep at least one 0-2 cost setup card and one Asuka Shikinami Langley (012) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.

Tech and flex

First cards to argue about

Mari Makinami Illustrious (UE15ST/EVA-1-111) are the first slots to shave if your room is all one matchup. Evangelion still has to respect Asuka UE15BT/EVA-1-051 and Spear of Gaius UE15BT/EVA-1-063 at one copy each.
