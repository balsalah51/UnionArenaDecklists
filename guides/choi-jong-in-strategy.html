Choi Jong-in strategy

Green Choi Jong-in in Solo Leveling is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 3. This site is hosting 36 public 50-card lists, including 6 recent top 8s. The tier list has Choi Jong-in in D.

How the raid face works

UE17BT/SLG-1-064 · cost 4 · 4000 BP · Green

The printed trigger on Choi Jong-in (UE17BT/SLG-1-064) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Choi Jong-in (UE17BT/SLG-1-064). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that deep redundancy on the Choi Jong-in line (063, 064, 062) means the deck reliably finds its namesake Raid target across multiple game states. The same snapshot flags that no standout Double Attack, Damage(2), or Impact payoff is visible in the list, capping the deck's ability to close games quickly once ahead.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Choi Jong-in, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Choi Jong-in (064) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.
