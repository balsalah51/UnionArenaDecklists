Yuna strategy

Purple Yuna in Sword Art Online is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 3. This site is hosting 66 public 50-card lists, including 11 recent top 8s. The tier list has Yuna in B.

How the raid face works

UEX06BT/SAO-2-032 · cost 3 · 3000 BP · Purple

The printed trigger on Yuna (UEX06BT/SAO-2-032) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Yuna (UEX06BT/SAO-2-032). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that highest consistency among rogue midrange raid decks via 5 Yuna printings. The same snapshot flags that no Impact/Damage-2 finisher unlike Tier 1/2 raid decks.

The curve

If your locals file plays a different 3-of, trust that file.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Yuna, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Yuna (032) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.

Tech and flex

First cards to argue about

Envy (UE09BT/FMA-1-076) and Yuna (UEX06BT/SAO-2-032) are the first slots to shave if your room is all one matchup.
