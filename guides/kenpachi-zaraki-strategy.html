Kenpachi Zaraki strategy

Green Kenpachi Zaraki in Bleach is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 3. This site is hosting 91 public 50-card lists, including 18 recent top 8s. The tier list has Kenpachi Zaraki in C.

How the raid face works

UEX01BT/BLC-2-067 · cost 4 · 4000 BP · Green

The printed trigger on Kenpachi Zaraki (UEX01BT/BLC-2-067) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Kenpachi Zaraki (UEX01BT/BLC-2-067) and Toshiro Hitsugaya (UE01BT/BLC-1-085). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that fully raided Kenpachi Zaraki plus Daiguren Hyorinmaru gives a genuine top-end power spike. The same snapshot flags that almost no proactive disruption, leaving it reactive until raid completion.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Kenpachi Zaraki, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Sajin Komamura (078) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.
