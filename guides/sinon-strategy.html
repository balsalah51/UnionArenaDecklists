Sinon strategy

Green Sinon in Sword Art Online is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as an aggro-tempo list, Standard tier 3. This site is hosting 105 public 50-card lists, including 13 recent top 8s. The tier list has Sinon in C.

How the raid face works

UEX06BT/SAO-2-073 · cost 6 · 4000 BP · Green

The printed trigger on Sinon (UEX06BT/SAO-2-073) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Kirito (UE07BT/SAO-1-087), Sinon (UEX06BT/SAO-2-073), and Sinon (UE07BT/SAO-1-091). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that snipe on the Sinon cards lets it attack specific characters, so it can bypass blockers and clear key defenders like those in Solo Leveling decks. The same snapshot flags that almost no recent top-cut presence suggests the deck is out-paced by faster aggro-tempo decks like Shin Asakura and Rem.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Sinon, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Kirito (086) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.

Tech and flex

First cards to argue about

Yuuki (UEPR/SAO-1-078) are the first slots to shave if your room is all one matchup.
