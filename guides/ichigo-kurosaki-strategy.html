Ichigo Kurosaki strategy

Purple Ichigo Kurosaki in Bleach is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 4. This site is hosting 108 public 50-card lists, including 6 recent top 8s. The tier list has Ichigo Kurosaki in D.

How the raid face works

UE01BT/BLC-1-041 · cost 5 · 4000 BP · Purple

The printed trigger on Ichigo Kurosaki (UE01BT/BLC-1-041) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Ichigo Kurosaki (UE01BT/BLC-1-041), Ichigo Kurosaki (UEX01BT/BLC-2-019), and Senjumaru Shutara (UEX01BT/BLC-2-057). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that deep redundancy across many Ichigo Kurosaki card numbers (016, 017, 018, 019, 039, 040, 041, 051, 103, 104) ensures the player almost always has a playable Ichigo on curve regardless of draw variance. The same snapshot flags that the deck lacks any visible Snipe, retire, or hand-disruption tools, making it purely reactive on board state against decks like CSM - Denji that can out-tempo it with aggressive removal-adjacent pressure.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Ichigo Kurosaki, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Bankai (066) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.
