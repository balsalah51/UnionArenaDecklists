Erwin Smith strategy

Blue Erwin Smith in Attack On Titan is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 3. This site is hosting 308 public 50-card lists, including 5 recent top 8s. The tier list has Erwin Smith in C.

How the raid face works

UE10ST/AOT-1-111 · cost 3 · 3500 BP · Blue

The printed trigger on Erwin Smith (UE10ST/AOT-1-111) is: [Draw] Draw a card. Raid printings in the current 50: Levi (UE10BT/AOT-1-011). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that the five-stage Levi raid line (007-011) allows the character to scale dramatically in stats and effects turn over turn once assembled, giving the deck a genuine late-game payoff. The same snapshot flags that committing to the full Levi raid chain across multiple turns cedes tempo to faster Tier 1 decks like CSM Denji and Rurouni Kenshin Hajime Saito, who can pressure life totals before the raid completes.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Erwin Smith, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Erwin Smith (002) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.
