Asuka Shikinami Langley strategy

Yellow Asuka Shikinami Langley in Evangelion is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as an aggro-tempo list, Standard tier 3. This site is hosting 444 public 50-card lists, including 14 recent top 8s. The tier list has Asuka Shikinami Langley in B.

How the raid face works

UE15BT/EVA-1-013 · cost 4 · 4000 BP · Yellow

The printed trigger on Asuka Shikinami Langley (UE15BT/EVA-1-013) is: [Get] Add this card to your hand. Raid printings in the current 50: Hellish Blizzard (UE06BT/OPM-1-023). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that asuka Shikinami Langley (012)/(013) provide redundant aggressive front-line bodies that let the deck apply pressure from turn one without waiting on setup pieces. The same snapshot flags that the deck has no observed tournament results in the current window, making its real-world viability against Tier 1 decks like Rurouni Kenshin - Hajime Saito and Solo Leveling - Sung Jinwoo purely speculative.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Asuka Shikinami Langley, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Hellish Blizzard (022) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.

Tech and flex

First cards to argue about

Hellish Blizzard (UE06BT/OPM-1-019), Hero (UE06ST/OPM-1-032), Encounter (UE14BT/NIK-1-029), and Asuka Shikinami Langley (UE15BT/EVA-1-013) are the first slots to shave if your room is all one matchup. Evangelion still has to respect Asuka UE15BT/EVA-1-051 and Spear of Gaius UE15BT/EVA-1-063 at one copy each.
