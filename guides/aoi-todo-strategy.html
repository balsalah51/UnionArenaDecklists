Aoi Todo strategy

Purple Aoi Todo in Jujutsu Kaisen is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 2. This site is hosting 156 public 50-card lists, including 30 recent top 8s. The tier list has Aoi Todo in A.

How the raid face works

UE03BT/JJK-1-081 · cost 5 · 4000 BP · Purple

The printed trigger on Aoi Todo (UE03BT/JJK-1-081) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Ultimate Mechamaru, Mode: Absolute (UEX02BT/JJK-3-027) and Aoi Todo (UE03BT/JJK-1-081). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that boogie Woogie's positional swap is a uniquely flexible tempo tool absent from other midrange decks. The same snapshot flags that multi-turn raid assembly is exploitable by the format's fastest aggro decks.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Aoi Todo, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Aoi Todo (080) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.
