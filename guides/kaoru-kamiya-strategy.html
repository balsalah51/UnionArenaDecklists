Kaoru Kamiya strategy

Red Kaoru Kamiya in Rurouni Kenshin is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 2. This site is hosting 103 public 50-card lists, including 37 recent top 8s. The tier list has Kaoru Kamiya in A.

How the raid face works

UE11ST/RNK-1-101 · cost 3 · 2500 BP · Red

The printed trigger on Kaoru Kamiya (UE11ST/RNK-1-101) is: [Draw] Draw a card. Raid printings in the current 50: Ayato Kirishima (UE18BT/TKG-1-075), Kotaro Amon (UE18BT/TKG-1-083), and Kenshin Himura (UE11ST/RNK-1-106). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that by far the highest meta share (7.7%) of any deck besides Sung Jinwoo, indicating strong real-world adoption not fully captured by static factor scores. The same snapshot flags that lower power ceiling (50) and disruption (38) than its Hajime Saito stablemate.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Kaoru Kamiya, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Kaoru Kamiya (101) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.
