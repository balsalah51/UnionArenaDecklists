Evangelion Production Model-08 strategy

Purple Evangelion Production Model-08 in Evangelion is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a tempo list, Standard tier 2. This site is hosting 89 public 50-card lists, including 7 recent top 8s. The tier list has Evangelion Production Model-08 in A.

How the raid face works

UE15BT/EVA-1-042 · cost 5 · 4000 BP · Purple

The printed trigger on Evangelion Production Model-08 (UE15BT/EVA-1-042) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Evangelion Test Type-01 (Spear of Gaius) (UE15BT/EVA-1-033), Evangelion Production Model-08 (UE15BT/EVA-1-042), Evangelion Production Model-02 (UE15BT/EVA-1-081), and Evangelion Production Model-New 02 (UE15BT/EVA-1-043). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that highest trigger quality (82) of any tempo deck in the field. The same snapshot flags that declining tournament share despite strong stats suggests the deck can't convert power into results.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Evangelion Production Model-08, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Mari Makinami Illustrious (056) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.

Tech and flex

First cards to argue about

Evangelion Test Type-01 (Spear of Gaius) (UE15BT/EVA-1-033) are the first slots to shave if your room is all one matchup. Evangelion still has to respect Asuka UE15BT/EVA-1-051 and Spear of Gaius UE15BT/EVA-1-063 at one copy each.
