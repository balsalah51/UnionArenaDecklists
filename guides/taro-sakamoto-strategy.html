Taro Sakamoto strategy

Yellow Taro Sakamoto in Sakamoto Days is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as an aggro list, Standard tier 2. This site is hosting 441 public 50-card lists, including 32 recent top 8s. The tier list has Taro Sakamoto in A.

How the raid face works

UE19BT/SMD-1-026 · cost 4 · 4000 BP · Yellow

The printed trigger on Taro Sakamoto (UE19BT/SMD-1-026) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Heisuke Mashimo (UE19BT/SMD-1-008), Shin Asakura (UE19BT/SMD-1-017), Taro Sakamoto (UE19BT/SMD-1-026), and Taro Sakamoto (UE19BT/SMD-1-025). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that near-elite tempo (82) and highest skill ceiling (80) in the entire dataset. The same snapshot flags that declining meta share (3.1%) despite excellent raw scores suggests it's losing ground to its Shin Asakura sibling or being teched against.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Taro Sakamoto, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Heisuke Mashimo (006) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.

Tech and flex

First cards to argue about
