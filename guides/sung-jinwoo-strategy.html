Sung Jinwoo strategy

Purple Sung Jinwoo in Solo Leveling is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a midrange list, Standard tier 1. This site is hosting 210 public 50-card lists, including 31 recent top 8s. The tier list has Sung Jinwoo in S.

How the raid face works

UE17BT/SLG-1-021 · cost 5 · 4000 BP · Purple

The printed trigger on Sung Jinwoo (UE17BT/SLG-1-021) is: [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it. Raid printings in the current 50: Sung Jinwoo (UE17BT/SLG-1-021) and Sung Jinwoo (UE17BT/SLG-1-020). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that the four-tier Sung Jinwoo raid chain (018→022→019→017, capped by Sung Jinwoo Winner) gives the deck a scaling threat that gets harder to answer each turn it survives. The same snapshot flags that against fast aggro like Sakamoto Days - Shin Asakura or Rurouni Kenshin - Hajime Saito, the raid chain can be too slow to assemble before life total collapses.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Sung Jinwoo, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Cha Hae-in (011) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.

Tech and flex
