Evangelion Proto Type-00 strategy

Red Evangelion Proto Type-00 in Evangelion is the raid face this page is about. TCG Contender files it as a tempo list, Standard tier 2. This site is hosting 131 public 50-card lists, including 9 recent top 8s. The tier list has Evangelion Proto Type-00 in A.

How the raid face works

UE15ST/EVA-1-105 · Red

Evangelion Proto Type-00 is the character the hosted 50s keep building around. Raid printings in the current 50: Garou (UE06BT/OPM-1-077). In Union Arena, [Raid] lets you add the card to your hand or perform Raid if the energy is there. The expensive copy is the ceiling. The cheaper copies with the same name are the steps. Contender's snapshot calls out that extremely high card-name redundancy (four copies of Evangelion Proto Type-00, Shinji Ikari 074/075, and three separate Rei Ayanami numbers) means the deck almost always opens with its core pieces available. The same snapshot flags that the list shows almost no dedicated removal or disruption tools, leaving it vulnerable to boards it cannot out-tempo, particularly against aggressive decks like Sakamoto Days - Shin Asakura or Rurouni Kenshin - Hajime Saito.

Mulligan

What the 50 implies

Keep a Raid Evangelion Proto Type-00, at least one 0-2 cost setup card, and one Garou (076) if the cheap card missed. Ship a hand of only 7+ cost finishers. That is inferred from costs in the hosted list, not a coach sheet from a hidden room.

Tech and flex

First cards to argue about

Garou (UE06BT/OPM-1-113) are the first slots to shave if your room is all one matchup. Evangelion still has to respect Asuka UE15BT/EVA-1-051 and Spear of Gaius UE15BT/EVA-1-063 at one copy each.
