How it plays

From public tournament lists

Demon Slayer - Muzan Kibutsuji (Geisha Form) is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The Muzan raid chain (Boy Form -> Geisha Form -> 035 SR) provides a genuine late-game power spike that can overwhelm boards lacking removal once fully assembled.. Redundant 4-ofs across Muzan's forms, Muzan's Blood, and Muzan's Research give the deck multiple paths to find raid pieces across a game..
