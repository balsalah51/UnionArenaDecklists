Rurouni Kenshin - Sojiro Seta

Rurouni Kenshin - Sojiro Seta is a tempo list. Standard tier 5 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The five distinct Sojiro Seta printings (055-059) enable raid-based scaling, letting the deck snowball stats and effects turn over turn if the sequencing is respected.. Makoto Shishio (043/044) paired with The Ten Swords Summoned gives the deck a thematic payoff engine that rewards assembling the Ten Swords cast on board..
