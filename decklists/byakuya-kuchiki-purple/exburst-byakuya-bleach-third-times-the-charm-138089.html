Characters

  • 4x Renji Abarai (045) UE01BT/BLC-1-045 Energy 0 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Byakuya Kuchiki (049) UE01BT/BLC-1-049 Energy 3 Buy
    [Color] Play one purple character card with 2 or less required energy and 1 AP cost from your sideline set to active onto your front line.
  • 4x Byakuya Kuchiki (050) UE01BT/BLC-1-050 Energy 5 Buy
    [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it.
  • 2x Renji Abarai UE01NC/BLC-3-005 Energy 5 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Byakuya Kuchiki UE01NC/BLC-3-006 Energy 1 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top three cards of your deck. Place up to one of them into your sideline, then place the remaining cards on the top of your deck in any order.
  • 4x Byakuya Kuchiki UEX01BT/BLC-2-053 Energy 2 Buy
  • 4x Renji Abarai UEX08BT/BLC-4-048 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top two cards of your deck. Place up to one purple <Renji Abarai> or <Rukia Kuchiki> card into your sideline. Place the remaining cards on the top of your deck in any order.
  • 4x Renji Abarai UEX08BT/BLC-4-050 Energy 4 Buy
    If you have paid an AP cost for an ability on one of your purple cards this turn, when you perform Raid with this card from your hand, reduce its AP cost by 1. If you have used another <Renji Abarai> card from your hand without paying AP this turn, this ability loses effect. [Raid] <Renji Abarai> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [Activate: Main] [If on the Front Line] [Once Per Turn] Choose one of the following: ・This character gains [Impact (1)] until the end of the turn. ・Choose one character on your opponent's front line. It loses 1000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Byakuya Kuchiki UEX08BT/BLC-4-051 Energy 3 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You can only activate this ability on the turn this character is played. Choose one of the following: ・Draw a card, then place one card from your hand into your sideline. ・If there is a character with BP less than its base BP on your opponent's front line, draw a card.
  • 4x Byakuya Kuchiki UEX08BT/BLC-4-052 Energy 0 Buy
    If there is a character with BP less than its base BP on your opponent's front line, this character gains 1500 BP.
  • 4x Byakuya Kuchiki UEX08BT/BLC-4-053 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Byakuya Kuchiki> Switch to active. May move to the front line. When this character attacks and wins a battle, switch it to active. You can only activate this <Byakuya Kuchiki> ability one time each turn. [During Your Turn] If there are three or more characters with BP less than their base BP on your opponent's front line, this character gains [Damage (2)] . [When Played] Choose up to one character on your opponent's front line. It loses 1000 BP until the end of the turn.

Events

  • 4x Senbonzakura Kageyoshi UE01BT/BLC-1-065 Energy 4 Buy
    [Special] Choose one character on your opponent's front line and sideline it.
  • 4x Bankai (066) UE01BT/BLC-1-066 Energy 3 Buy
    [Final] If you have 0 life, place the top card of your deck into your life area.
