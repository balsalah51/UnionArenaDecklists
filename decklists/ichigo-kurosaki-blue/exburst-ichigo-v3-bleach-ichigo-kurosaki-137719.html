  • 4x Ichigo Kurosaki (019) UEX01BT/BLC-2-019 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it.
  • 3x Zangetsu UEX01BT/BLC-2-021 Energy 0 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 2x Orihime Inoue UEX08BT/BLC-4-022 Energy 4 Buy
    Play this character set to active. [When Played] You may place one card from your hand into your sideline. If you do, add up to one blue character card with 1 or less required energy from your sideline to your hand, or play it set to resting onto your field. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You can only activate this ability on the turn this character is played. Choose one character on your field. It gains "This character cannot be chosen by your opponent's abilities" until the start of your next turn.
  • 4x Kisuke Urahara UEX08BT/BLC-4-024 Energy 3 Buy
    [When Played] Choose up to one other character with no affinities on your field and move it to the other line.
  • 4x Ichigo Kurosaki UEX08BT/BLC-4-027 Energy 3 Buy
    [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] Place one card with "Zangetsu" or "Getsuga" in its card name from your hand into your sideline. If you do, draw a card and give this character 1500 BP, " [#If on the Energy Line#] At the start of your attack phase, if your front line is not full, move this character to your front line," and "This character cannot be chosen by trigger abilities" until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Yoruichi Shihoin UEX08BT/BLC-4-030 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] You may place one card from your hand into your sideline. If you do, look at the top card of your deck, place it on the top or bottom of your deck, then draw a card.
  • 4x Yoruichi Shihoin UEX08BT/BLC-4-032 Energy 3 Buy
    [During Your Turn] If <Ichigo Kurosaki> or <Kisuke Urahara> is on your field, this character gains 1000 BP. [#If on the Energy Line#] [Once Per Turn] At the end of one of your character's attacks, choose one character on your front line that has not attacked this turn. You may swap it with this character.
  • 4x Yoruichi Shihoin UEX08BT/BLC-4-033 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Yoruichi Shihoin> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [When Played] Choose up to one other character with no affinities on your field and switch it to active. [When Sidelined] If <Kisuke Urahara> is on your field, play up to one base card of this Raided character set to resting onto your field.
