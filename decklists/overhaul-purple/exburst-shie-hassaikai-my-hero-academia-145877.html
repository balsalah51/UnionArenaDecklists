    [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) [When Sidelined] Draw a card, then place one card from your hand into your sideline.

    [When Played] You may place the top three cards of your deck into your sideline. [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Sideline This Card] Add one character card with [Shie Hassaikai] affinity or one <Quirk-Destroying Bullet> card from your sideline to your hand.
  • 2x Stain UE25BT/MHA-1-011 Energy 4 Buy
    Characters that block this character's attacks will remain set to resting the next time they would be switched to active. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] This character gains "Your opponent must block this character's attacks if able" until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Overhaul UE25BT/MHA-1-024 Energy 0 Buy
    -
  • 4x Overhaul UE25BT/MHA-1-025 Energy 1 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top four cards of your deck. Reveal up to one character card with [Shie Hassaikai] affinity or one <Eri> card and add it to your hand. Place the remaining cards on the bottom of your deck in any order. If you added a card to your hand, place one card from your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x Overhaul UE25BT/MHA-1-026 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Overhaul> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [Impact (1)] (When this character attacks and wins a battle, deal 1 damage to your opponent.) [When Played] You may place the top three cards of your deck into your sideline. If you do, add up to one character card with [Shie Hassaikai] affinity other than <Overhaul> or one <Eri> card from your sideline to your hand.
  • 4x Chronostasis UE25BT/MHA-1-027 Energy 3 Buy
    If <Overhaul> is on your front line, this character gains 1000 BP.
  • 4x Mimic UE25BT/MHA-1-028 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] Draw a card, then place one card from your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x Overhaul UE25BT/MHA-2-047 Energy 3 Buy
    [When Sidelined] Draw a card.
  • 4x Kendo Rappa UE25BT/MHA-2-048 Energy 4 Buy
    If you have six or more [Shie Hassaikai] affinity cards in your sideline, this character gains [Damage (2)] (Direct damage dealt by this character's attacks deals 2 damage.)

Events

  • 4x Shie Hassaikai UE25BT/MHA-1-031 Buy
    Add up to one character card with [Shie Hassaikai] affinity from your sideline to your hand. Play up to one character card with [Shie Hassaikai] affinity, fulfilled required energy, and 1 AP cost from your hand set to resting onto your field, or perform Raid with it.
  • 4x We'll Do What We Want UE25BT/MHA-1-032 Buy
    Choose up to two of your AP cards and switch them to active.

Sites

  • 4x Quirk-Destroying Bullet UE25BT/MHA-1-029 Buy
    You cannot use this card unless <Overhaul> is on your field. [When Played] Choose up to one character on your opponent's front line. Switch it to resting and place all cards under it into their sideline. It loses all base abilities and will not switch to active while this site is on your field.
