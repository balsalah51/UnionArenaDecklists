How it plays

From public tournament lists

Attack On Titan - Levi is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on core pieces: four copies each of Levi (007), (008), (009), (010), and (011) alongside Erwin Smith (001) and (002) means the deck rarely whiffs on finding its namesake characters.. The Raid chain built around Levi stacking onto itself across five printings allows incremental stat and ability upgrades turn over turn if sequenced correctly..
