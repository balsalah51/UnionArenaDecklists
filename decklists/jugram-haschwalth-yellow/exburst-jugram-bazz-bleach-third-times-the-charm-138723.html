Characters

  • 4x Jugram Haschwalth (018) UE01BT/BLC-1-018 Energy 0 Buy
    [Get] Add this card to your hand.
  • 4x Jugram Haschwalth (020) UE01BT/BLC-1-020 Energy 5 Buy
    [Raid] Add this card to your hand, or if you have the required energy, perform Raid with it.
  • 4x Bazz UEX08BT/BLC-4-001 Energy 1 Buy
    This card is also treated as <Bazz-B>. [When Played] Look at the top three cards of your deck. Reveal up to one <Bazz-B> or <Jugram Haschwalth> card and add it to your hand. Place the remaining cards on the bottom of your deck in any order. If you added a card to your hand, place one card from your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x Jugo UEX08BT/BLC-4-002 Energy 0 Buy
    This card is also treated as <Jugram Haschwalth>. [When Played] Look at the top card of your deck, then place it on the top of your deck or into your sideline.
  • 4x Askin Nakk Le Vaar UEX08BT/BLC-4-003 Energy 2 Buy
    If you have no cards on your field, reduce this card's required energy by [Yellow×2] while in your hand. [Step] (During your movement phase, you may move this card from your front line to your energy line.) [When Sidelined] If it is your turn, choose up to one character with 1500 or more BP on your opponent's front line. It loses 1000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Bazz-B UEX08BT/BLC-4-006 Energy 3 Buy
    [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) [During Your Turn] If <Yhwach> is not in your sideline and <Jugram Haschwalth> is on your field, this character gains 1000 BP.
  • 3x Bazz-B UEX08BT/BLC-4-007 Energy 4 Buy
    [Activate: Main] You can only activate this ability if <Yhwach> is not in your sideline and this character's required energy is 5 or more. Switch this character to active. You can only activate this <Bazz-B> ability one time each turn.
  • 4x Bazz-B UEX08BT/BLC-4-008 Energy 4 Buy
    [When Played] If <Yhwach> is not in your sideline, you may sideline one other <Bazz-B> on your field. If you do, choose up to one character on your opponent's front line with BP equal to or less than 1000 multiplied by the required energy value of the sidelined character and sideline it, then switch this character to active. If you sidelined one of your characters with 5 or more required energy with this effect, this character gains [Impact (1)] until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Jugram Haschwalth UEX08BT/BLC-4-011 Energy 3 Buy
    [When Played] Draw a card, then place one card from your hand into your sideline. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You can only activate this ability on the turn this character is played. Choose one other character on your field. Its required energy increases by 2 until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Jugram Haschwalth UEX08BT/BLC-4-012 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Jugram Haschwalth> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [If on the Front Line] When another character on your field is sidelined, draw up to one card. You can only activate this <Jugram Haschwalth> ability one time each turn. [During Your Turn] [If on the Front Line] The required energy of all other characters on your field increases by 2. [When Played] If the cards on your field have no affinities other than [Stern Ritters], play up to one yellow character card with 2 or less required energy and 1 AP cost from your hand set to resting onto your field.
  • 4x Yhwach UEX08BT/BLC-4-015 Energy 5 Buy
    When this card is placed into your sideline, place it from your sideline onto the bottom of your deck. [Activate: Main] [If on the Front Line] [Once Per Turn] Sideline one other character on your field. If you do, switch this character to active and look at the top three cards of your deck. Add one card to your hand. Place the remaining cards on the bottom of your deck in any order. If you sidelined one of your characters with 4 or more required energy with this effect, this character gains 1000 BP until the start of your next turn.

Events
