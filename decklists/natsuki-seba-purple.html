How it plays

From public tournament lists

Natsuki Seba (Purple) is a midrange-control list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The five-copy Natsuki Seba character core (065/066/067/068/069) gives the deck a thick, redundant engine so the key character is reliably on board by mid-game.. Tanaka and Kashima as full 4-ofs provide a stable supporting front line that can be built up turn over turn to feed the energy line consistently..
