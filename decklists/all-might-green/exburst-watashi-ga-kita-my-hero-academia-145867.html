  • 4x All Might UE25BT/MHA-1-102 Energy 4 Buy
    [Damage (2)] (Direct damage dealt by this character's attacks deals 2 damage.) [During Your Turn] This character gains 1000 BP. [When Played] [If on the Front Line] This character gains 1000 BP until the start of your next turn.
  • 4x Izuku Midoriya UE25BT/MHA-1-106 Energy 3 Buy
    If there are three or more characters on your opponent's front line, this character gains 1000 BP.
  • 4x Izuku Midoriya UE25BT/MHA-1-107 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Izuku Midoriya> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [Impact (1)] (When this character attacks and wins a battle, deal 1 damage to your opponent.) [When Played] If there are three or more characters on your opponent's front line, draw a card.
  • 4x All Might UE25BT/MHA-2-054 Energy 6 Buy
    If there are three or more characters on your opponent's front line, reduce this card's required energy by [Green×4] while in your hand. This card cannot be played onto your energy line. If this character has 6000 or more BP, it gains [Impact (1)] . [When Played] If this character is played with one of your abilities, it gains 1000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Izuku Midoriya UE25BT/MHA-3-006 Energy 0 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] You can only activate this ability if there are three or more characters on your opponent's front line. Choose one other character on your field. It gains 500 BP until the end of the turn. If the chosen character is <Izuku Midoriya>, it also gains "If this character has 5000 or more BP, it gains [Impact (1)] " until the end of the turn. You can only activate this <Izuku Midoriya> ability one time each turn.

Events

  • 4x Detroit Smash UE25BT/MHA-1-063 Buy
    Choose one character on your field. It gains 2500 BP and "When this character attacks and wins a battle, draw a card" until the end of the turn. If the chosen character is <All Might> or <Izuku Midoriya>, it also gains [Snipe] (You may target a character on your opponent's front line and attack it with this character. If you do, your opponent cannot block) until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Plus Ultra UE25BT/MHA-1-065 Buy
    Choose up to two of your AP cards and switch them to active.
  • 4x I Am Here! UE25BT/MHA-1-066 Buy
    Look at the top three cards of your deck. Reveal up to one character card and add it to your hand. Place the remaining cards on the bottom of your deck in any order. Play up to one green character card from your hand set to resting onto your field. If there are three or more characters on your opponent's front line and the character you play is <All Might>, switch it to active.
