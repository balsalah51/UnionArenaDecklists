How it plays

From public tournament lists

Kaiju No. 8 (Green) is an aggro-tempo list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The raid chain across Kikoru Shinomiya (016, 020, 018, 019) lets the deck continually upgrade a single board slot's BP and abilities without spending extra AP on a fresh character, a strong tempo loop early.. Three distinct Reno Ichikawa printings (009, 010, 012) plus 4x Soshiro Hoshina give the energy line cheap, redundant fuel so the deck reliably hits its color/energy thresholds by turn two or three..
