Characters

  • 4x Scarlet (050) UE14BT/NIK-1-050 Energy 0 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Snow White (055) UE14BT/NIK-1-055 Energy 4 Buy
    [Color] Play one purple character card with 2 or less required energy and 1 AP cost from your sideline set to active onto your front line.
  • 4x Rapunzel (056) UE14BT/NIK-1-056 Energy 0 Buy
    [Get] Add this card to your hand.
  • 2x Modernia UE14BT/NIK-1-060 Energy 2 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Scarlet (110) UE14BT/NIK-1-110 Energy 3 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Rapunzel (002) UE14BT/NIK-P-002 Energy 1 Buy
  • 4x Crown UP01BT/NIK-2-020 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top two cards of your deck. Place up to one [White Knight] affinity, [Pioneer] affinity, or <Modernia> card into your sideline. Place the remaining cards on the top of your deck in any order.
  • 4x Crown UP01BT/NIK-2-021 Energy 2 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top three cards of your deck. Reveal up to one [White Knight] affinity, [Pioneer] affinity, or <Modernia> card and add it to your hand. Place the remaining cards on the bottom of your deck in any order. If you added a card to your hand, place one card from your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x Crown UP01BT/NIK-2-022 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Crown> / <Trombe> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [When Played] Draw a card. Then, you may place one card from your hand into your sideline. If you do, play up to one purple [White Knight] affinity, [Pioneer] affinity, or <Modernia> card with 3 or less required energy and 1 AP cost from your hand set to resting onto your field. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] This character gains 1000 BP and [Impact (1)] until the end of the turn. Place all the cards in your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x Trombe UP01BT/NIK-2-023 Energy 3 Buy
    [When Played] If <Crown> is on your field, choose one of the following: ・Draw a card, then place one card from your hand into your sideline. ・If you have placed a card from your hand into your sideline with one of your abilities this turn, draw a card.
  • 4x Scarlet UP01BT/NIK-2-037 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Scarlet> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) [Activate: Main] [Sideline This Card] Play up to one purple [Pioneer] affinity card with 5 or less required energy from your hand set to active onto your field. [When Sidelined] Play up to one base card of this Raided character set to resting onto your field.

Events

  • 4x Encounter UE14BT/NIK-1-063 Energy 3 Buy
    [Final] If you have 0 life, place the top card of your deck into your life area.
  • 4x Render Heaven and Earth Asunder! UE14BT/NIK-1-064 Energy 4 Buy
    [Special] Choose one character on your opponent's front line and sideline it.
