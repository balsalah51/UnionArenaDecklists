    This card is also treated as <Rapunzel>. [When Attacking] If you have three or more other [Goddess] affinity cards whose original card names are unique on your field, you may draw a card. If you do, place one card from your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x Red Hood UP01BT/NIK-2-033 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] Choose up to {one} [Goddess] affinity card on your field. It gains 1000 BP until the end of the turn. If this character is played by an ability on one of your <Red Hood> cards, {two} instead.
  • 4x Red Hood UP01BT/NIK-2-034 Energy 3 Buy
    [When Played] You may return one other [Goddess] affinity card on your field with 3 or less required energy to your hand. If you do, choose up to one [Goddess] affinity card on your field. {It gains 1000 BP until the end of the turn}. If this character is played by an ability on one of your <Red Hood> cards, {Switch it to active and give it 1000 BP until the end of the turn} instead.
  • 4x Red Hood UP01BT/NIK-2-035 Energy 2 Buy
    If you have three or more other [Goddess] affinity cards whose original card names are unique on your field, this character gains [Purple] energy generation. [When Played] If this character is played by an ability on one of your <Red Hood> cards, you gain "If <Red Hood> is on your front line, your opponent cannot choose characters on your field other than <Red Hood> with abilities unless they place one card from their hand into their sideline as an additional cost" until the start of your next turn.
  • 4x Red Hood UP01BT/NIK-2-036 Energy 5 Buy
    If this character has 6000 or more BP, it gains [Impact (1)] . [Raid] <Red Hood> Switch to active. May move to the front line. At the end of this character's attack, play up to one base card of this Raided character set to resting onto your field. [When Played] This character gains "This character cannot be chosen by your opponent's trigger abilities" until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Scarlet UP01BT/NIK-2-037 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Scarlet> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) [Activate: Main] [Sideline This Card] Play up to one purple [Pioneer] affinity card with 5 or less required energy from your hand set to active onto your field. [When Sidelined] Play up to one base card of this Raided character set to resting onto your field.
