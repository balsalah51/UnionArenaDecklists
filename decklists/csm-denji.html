Chainsaw Man - Denji is an aggro list. Standard tier 1 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across Denji variants (003/004/006/008) and Reze variants (025/026/028/030/031) means the deck rarely misses its early aggressive curve.. Bomb Devil (016/017) provides explosive burst damage that can punish opponents who stabilize too slowly against the early swarm..

From public tournament lists

Chainsaw Man - Denji is an aggro list. Standard tier 1 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot.
