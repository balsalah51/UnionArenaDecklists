Evangelion - Evangelion 13

Evangelion - Evangelion 13 is a tempo list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on Kaworu Nagisa across four distinct printings (037/038/039 plus base) means the deck reliably finds its central synergy piece even through mulligans.. Multiple Shinji Ikari variants (028/029/031) allow flexible energy-line or front-line deployment depending on matchup needs..
