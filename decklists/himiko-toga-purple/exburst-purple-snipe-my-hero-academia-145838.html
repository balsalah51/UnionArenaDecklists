  • 4x Gentle Criminal UE25BT/MHA-1-007 Energy 3 Buy
    [Impact (1)] (When this character attacks and wins a battle, deal 1 damage to your opponent.) [During Your Turn] Your opponent's characters that lose to this character in battle return to their hand instead of being sidelined.
  • 2x Stain UE25BT/MHA-1-011 Energy 4 Buy
    Characters that block this character's attacks will remain set to resting the next time they would be switched to active. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] This character gains "Your opponent must block this character's attacks if able" until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Twice UE25BT/MHA-1-015 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Sidelined] Draw a card.
  • 4x Himiko Toga UE25BT/MHA-1-018 Energy 0 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] This character gains "When this character attacks and wins a battle, draw a card" until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Himiko Toga UE25BT/MHA-1-019 Energy 1 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [If on the Front Line] [Once Per Turn] Choose one character with 1500 or more BP on your opponent's front line. It loses 1000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x La Brava UE25BT/MHA-1-023 Energy 2 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [If on the Front Line] [Once Per Turn] Choose one other character on your field. It gains {1000 BP} until the end of the turn. If you do, your opponent cannot block)} instead.
  • 4x Himiko Toga UE25BT/MHA-2-042 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Himiko Toga> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [When Played] Choose up to one character on your opponent's front line. It loses 1000 BP until the end of the turn. [Activate: Main] [If on the Front Line] [Once Per Turn] Choose one character on your opponent's front line. It loses 1000 BP until the end of the turn. This character gains 1000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Lady Nagant UE25BT/MHA-2-044 Energy 0 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Pay 1 AP] [Once Per Turn] Draw a card.
  • 4x Lady Nagant UE25BT/MHA-2-045 Energy 3 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Place 2 Cards From Hand Into Sideline] [Once Per Turn] This character gains [Snipe] (You may target a character on your opponent's front line and attack it with this character. If you do, your opponent cannot block) and "This character cannot target characters with 3000 or more BP using [Snipe] " until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Lady Nagant UE25BT/MHA-2-046 Energy 6 Buy
    [Raid] <Lady Nagant> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [When Played] This character gains [Snipe] (You may target a character on your opponent's front line and attack it with this character. If you do, your opponent cannot block) until the end of the turn. [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Once Per Turn] This character gains 1000 BP and [Impact (1)] until the end of the turn. [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Once Per Turn] This character gains " [When Attacking] Draw a card" until the end of the turn. [Activate: Main] [Place 1 Card From Hand Into Sideline] Switch this character to active.
  • 4x Himiko Toga UE25BT/MHA-3-004 Energy 3 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] You can only activate this ability if there is a character with BP less than its base BP on your opponent's field. Choose one character with no affinities on your field. It gains " [When Attacking] Draw up to one card" until the end of the turn. You can only activate this <Himiko Toga> ability one time each turn. (You can only activate it one time, including on another character that has gained this effect.)
