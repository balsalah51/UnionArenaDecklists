How it plays

From public tournament lists

Hunter x Hunter - Gon Freecss is an aggro-tempo list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on the Gon Freecss line (nine distinct printings at up to 4 copies) means the core character is almost always accessible regardless of mulligan or draw order.. Killua Zoldyck at 4 copies provides a consistent secondary beater/support piece that can fill the front line while Gon pieces assemble on the energy line..
