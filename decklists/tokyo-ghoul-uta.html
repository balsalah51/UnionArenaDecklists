How it plays

From public tournament lists

Tokyo Ghoul - Uta is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The Ken Kaneki raid chain (038, 039, 040, 041, 042) rewards careful sequencing and lets the deck snowball board presence once the transformation line is assembled.. Hinami Fueguchi and Nishiki Nishio provide efficient early energy-line bodies that support the higher-cost Kaneki and Shu Tsukiyama plays without stalling tempo..
