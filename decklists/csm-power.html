Chainsaw Man - Power

Chainsaw Man - Power: 121 public 50-card Standard lists · newest 2026-09-24 · Card pictures and TCGplayer links

Chainsaw Man - Power is an aggro list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Explosive early board presence via Power (057/059/060/061) allows the deck to apply pressure before opponents stabilize, especially against slower midrange decks like Bleach - Kenny.. Denji (049/050/051) provides scalable BP threats that can close games quickly once raided or evolved, giving the deck a real power ceiling in the mid-game..
