Chainsaw Man - Power

Chainsaw Man - Power: 38 public 50-card Standard lists · newest 2026-09-11 · Card pictures and TCGplayer links

Chainsaw Man - Power is an aggro list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Dense low-to-mid cost character curve (Power 057/060/061, Chainsaw Man 047/048) allows the deck to commit multiple bodies to the front line by turn 2-3, generating early pressure most midrange decks in the format can't match.. Denji (049/050/051) offers redundant aggressive threats that keep the clock running even if one copy gets answered, smoothing out variance in the aggro plan..
