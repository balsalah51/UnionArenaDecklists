How it plays

From public tournament lists

Mina Ashiro (Yellow) is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Triple-printed Reno Ichikawa (009/010/012) gives the deck unusual redundancy on its namesake threat, making it hard to be starved of the core piece across a long tournament day.. Mina Ashiro's five total copies (001-005) form a cheap, reliable energy-line backbone that lets the deck hit its color requirements on schedule through the 1/2/3 AP ramp..
