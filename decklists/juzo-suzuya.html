How it plays

From public tournament lists

Tokyo Ghoul - Juzo Suzuya is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on the identity card - four separate Kisho Arima printings (002/003/004 plus the base) mean the deck almost always has a copy to develop early and chain into later Raid upgrades.. Triple Juzo Suzuya variants (007/008/009/010) create a dense secondary engine that can restock board presence turn after turn if one copy gets sidelined or answered..
