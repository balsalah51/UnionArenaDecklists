How it plays

From public tournament lists

Aoi Todo (Purple) is a midrange list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Heavy redundancy across four separate Aoi Todo printings (main, 079, 081, 080) means the deck almost always has a Todo body available regardless of which copy is drawn.. 4x Kokichi Muta (029) into Ultimate Mechamaru and Ultimate Mechamaru, Mode: Absolute gives a reliable upgrade path from a cheap early body into a powerful late-game threat..
