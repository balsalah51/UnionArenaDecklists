How it plays

From public tournament lists

Cinderella (Yellow) is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Four-copy Cinderella and Grave give reliable key pieces. Beats slow control-leaning lists.

Yellow sample list

One picture per card is in the gallery below. Copy pastes NxSET/CODE lines.

Characters

  • 4x Marian UE14BT/NIK-1-007 Energy 0 Buy
    [Draw] Draw a card.
  • 4x Anis (017) UE14BT/NIK-1-017 Energy 3 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Abe UP01BT/NIK-2-001 Energy 0 Buy
    This card is also treated as <Grave>. [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] You can only activate this ability if you have 10 or more cards in your removal area. Place this character into your removal area and draw a card. Reduce the required energy of the next <Cinderella> card with 10 or more base required energy you use from your hand this turn by [Yellow×6] .
  • 4x Grave UP01BT/NIK-2-009 Energy 1 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top three cards of your deck. Reveal up to one [Old Tales] affinity card and add it to your hand. Place the remaining cards on the bottom of your deck in any order. If you added a card to your hand, place one card from your hand into your removal area.
  • 4x Grave UP01BT/NIK-2-010 Energy 4 Buy
    [When Played] If this character is played from your hand, you may pay 1 AP. If you do, draw a card. Then, you may place one <Cinderella> card from your hand into your removal area. If you do, activate its [Trigger] ability.
  • 4x Grave UP01BT/NIK-2-011 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Grave> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [When Played] Place the top two cards of your deck into your removal area. You may place one card from your hand into your removal area. If you do, play up to one yellow card with {0} or less required energy, 1 AP cost, and [Old Tales] affinity from your removal area set to resting onto your field. If it is your turn, for every card in your removal area, add 1 to the required energy range of the character you may play with this ability.
  • 4x Cinderella UP01BT/NIK-2-012 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top two cards of your deck. Place up to one card into your removal area. Place the remaining cards on the top of your deck in any order.
  • 4x Cinderella UP01BT/NIK-2-013 Energy 2 Buy
    [When Played] If you have three or more cards in your removal area, draw a card.
  • 4x Cinderella UP01BT/NIK-2-014 Energy 4 Buy
    [Raid] <Cinderella> Switch to active. May move to the front line. [When Played] Choose up to one [Old Tales] affinity card on your field and switch it to active. [When Sidelined] Look at the top three cards of your deck. Add one card to your hand. Place the remaining cards into your removal area.
  • 4x Cinderella UP01BT/NIK-2-015 Energy 10 Buy
    [Special] Choose one character on your opponent's front line and sideline it.
  • 2x Little Mermaid UP01BT/NIK-2-016 Energy 3 Buy
    [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) [During Your Turn] If you have three or more cards in your removal area, this character gains 1000 BP. [When Sidelined] Place this card into your removal area.
  • 4x Little Mermaid UP01BT/NIK-2-017 Energy 4 Buy
    [Double Block] (When this character blocks for the first time this turn, switch it to active.) [When Attacking] Look at the top card of your deck, then place it on the top of your deck or into your removal area. Then, if there are six or more cards in your removal area, draw a card. [When Sidelined] Place this card into your removal area.

Events

  • 4x Encounter UE14BT/NIK-1-029 Energy 3 Buy
    [Final] If you have 0 life, place the top card of your deck into your life area.
