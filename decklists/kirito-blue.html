From public tournament lists

Kirito (Blue) is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across five Yuna numbers (028-032) means the core swarm plan is rarely dead in hand, supporting a stable early energy line.. Agil and Silica provide cheap support bodies that help the deck reach its energy thresholds ahead of curve compared to top-heavy midrange decks..
