How it plays

From public tournament lists

Ken Kaneki (Purple) is a midrange list. Standard tier 5 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across five separate Ken Kaneki printings (038, 039, 040, 041, 042) means the deck rarely misses its core character on curve, smoothing out mulligan variance.. Hinami Fueguchi at 4 copies provides a stable low-cost energy line piece to enable Yoshimura and Shu Tsukiyama's likely higher energy requirements later..
