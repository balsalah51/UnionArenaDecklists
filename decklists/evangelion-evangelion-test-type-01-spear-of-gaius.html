Evangelion - Evangelion Test Type-01 (Spear of Gaius)

Evangelion - Evangelion Test Type-01 (Spear of Gaius) is a tempo list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy in the Shinji Ikari line (027, 029, 031 at 4 copies each) ensures the deck rarely misses its core character engine even through mulligans.. The raid potential with 4x Evangelion Test Type-01 (Spear of Gaius) plus 2x the Spear of Gaius event card suggests a strong evolved-stat payoff for players who set up the raid correctly..
