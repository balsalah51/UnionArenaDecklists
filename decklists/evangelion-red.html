Evangelion - Red

Evangelion - Red is a midrange list. Standard tier 5 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on core pieces — four copies each of Rei Ayanami (069, 070, 071, 102) and Shinji Ikari (074, 075) — ensures the deck reliably assembles its board plan even through mulligans.. Misato Katsuragi (084) and (085) provide support pieces that can stabilize the mid-game while the Ayanami/Ikari core develops..
