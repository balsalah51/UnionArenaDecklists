Evangelion - Mari Makinami Illustrious

Evangelion - Mari Makinami Illustrious is a tempo list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep four-of redundancy on Rei Ayanami (004/005/110), Mari Makinami (014/017/018), and Misato Katsuragi (006/008) means the deck rarely misses its core pieces even through mulligans.. The event suite (A Hot Bath Lets You Wash Your Life and Begin Anew, What Kind of Idiot Are You?) provides resource smoothing that keeps the hand stocked through the mid-game grind..
