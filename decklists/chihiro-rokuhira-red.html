How it plays

From public tournament lists

Chihiro Rokuhira (Red) is an aggro-tempo list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across four separate Iori Samura printings (006, 004, 005, base) lets the deck reliably flood its front line by turn two even on the draw.. Yoji Uruha copies (base and 018) provide early energy-line bodies that help the deck meet Chihiro Rokuhira's energy thresholds on curve..
