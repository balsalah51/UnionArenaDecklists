Evangelion - Evangelion Production Model-08

Evangelion - Evangelion Production Model-08 is a tempo list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The Production Model-08 raid engine, backed by 4x copies each of the 02 and New 02 variants, allows consistent evolution into stronger stat lines turn over turn.. Asuka Shikinami Langley's four distinct printings (048, 049, 050, 113) give the deck multiple angles of aggression and redundancy, making it hard to disrupt a single key piece..
