How it plays

From public tournament lists

Jujutsu Kaisen - Yuji Itadori is an aggro-tempo list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Multiple redundant copies of Yuji Itadori across different printings (013, 037, 038, 041, 042, 043, 3-040) let the deck consistently find a front-line threat on curve even through mulligans.. Black Flash at a full 4 copies gives reliable combat-trick density to swing BP battles favorably and protect attackers from unfavorable trades..
