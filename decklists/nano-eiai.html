How it plays

From public tournament lists

100 Girlfriends - Nano Eiai is a combo-midrange list. Standard tier 5 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Extremely dense core package - four copies each across five distinct Nano Eiai card numbers (047, 049, 050, 052, 053) ensures the deck's central character is drawn and playable almost every game.. Multiple Shizuka Yoshimoto printings (061, 062, 065, 066, 067) give the deck layered synergy pieces that can be sequenced differently depending on the opening hand..
