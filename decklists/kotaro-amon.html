How it plays

From public tournament lists

Tokyo Ghoul - Kotaro Amon is a midrange list. Standard tier 5 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy in the raid chain: 16 total copies across Kotaro Amon (080/081/082/083) make it very likely to draw and progress the core raid line by mid-game.. Juzo Suzuya is similarly supported with 12 copies across three printings, giving a reliable secondary engine even if the Amon line is disrupted..
