How it plays

From public tournament lists

Historia Reiss (Blue) is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across four distinct Historia Reiss printings (019-022) means the deck reliably finds its namesake card regardless of which copy is drawn.. Multiple Ymir variants (012, 013, and Jaw Titan) give flexible energy-line or front-line bodies to build a stable board before committing to bigger plays..
