Rurouni Kenshin - Purple

Rurouni Kenshin - Purple is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The four-stage Kenshin Himura raid line (045-048) offers a scaling threat that can overwhelm boards once fully assembled, punishing opponents who can't remove the base copy early.. Hajime Saito's six distinct printings (035-040 plus the rare 113) give the deck multiple stat and ability profiles to adapt its front-line composition to different matchups..
