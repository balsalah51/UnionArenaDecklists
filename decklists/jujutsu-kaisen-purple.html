Jujutsu Kaisen - Purple

Jujutsu Kaisen - Purple is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Multiple Aoi Todo variants (079/080/081 plus base) give redundancy for the deck's key beatstick, reducing dead draws when one copy is removed or stuck in hand.. Ultimate Mechamaru into Ultimate Mechamaru, Mode: Absolute provides a scalable threat line that can close games once the Domain Expansion support is online..
