Sakamoto Days - Nagumo

Sakamoto Days - Nagumo is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep raid redundancy on Taro Sakamoto (047/049/051) and Nagumo (057/058/060/061) means the deck can consistently find and evolve its core threats even through disruption.. Osaragi's three variants (042/044/046) provide layered utility that can adapt to different board states, giving the deck some flexibility within its midrange shell..
