How it plays

From public tournament lists

Black Clover - Asta is an aggro-tempo list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Heavy redundancy of Asta character variants (002, 003, 004, 044, 046, 073, 075, 102, 103, 110) means the deck almost always has a playable 0-2 energy body to start the energy line on turn one.. Black Bulls as a recurring support/named card suggests a Raid chain building from base Asta into stronger upgraded versions, letting the deck snowball board presence across the 1/2/3 AP ramp..
