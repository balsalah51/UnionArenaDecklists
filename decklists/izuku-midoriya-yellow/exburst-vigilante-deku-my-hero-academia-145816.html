Characters

  • 4x All Might UE25BT/MHA-2-002 Energy 1 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top four cards of your deck. Reveal up to one <Izuku Midoriya> or [One For All] affinity card and add it to your hand. Place the remaining cards on the bottom of your deck in any order. If you added a card to your hand, place one card from your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x All Might UE25BT/MHA-2-003 Energy 2 Buy
    If you have a combined total of three or more [One For All] affinity cards with unique card names on your field and in your sideline, this character gains [Yellow] energy generation. [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] Place one [One For All] affinity card from your hand into your sideline. If you do, draw a card.
  • 4x Izuku Midoriya UE25BT/MHA-2-022 Energy 3 Buy
    If you have a [Class A] affinity card other than <Izuku Midoriya> on your field, this card cannot be played from your hand. [Step] (During your movement phase, you may move this card from your front line to your energy line.) If you have a combined total of three or more [One For All] affinity cards with unique card names on your field and in your sideline, this character gains 1000 BP.
  • 4x Izuku Midoriya UE25BT/MHA-2-024 Energy 4 Buy
    If you have a [Class A] affinity card other than <Izuku Midoriya> on your field, this card cannot be played from your hand. [When Played] This character gains " [Once Per Turn] When a [One For All] affinity card on your field is sidelined, draw up to one card" until the end of the turn. [When Attacking] If you have a combined total of three or more [One For All] affinity cards with unique card names on your field and in your sideline, you may pay 1 AP. If you do, this character gains [Impact (1)] (When this character attacks and wins a battle, deal 1 damage to your opponent) until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Izuku Midoriya UE25BT/MHA-2-026 Energy 8 Buy
    If you have a [Class A] affinity card other than <Izuku Midoriya> on your field, this card cannot be played from your hand. For each [One For All] affinity card with a unique card name on your field and in your sideline, reduce this card's required energy by [Yellow×1] while in your hand. [Impact (1)] (When this character attacks and wins a battle, deal 1 damage to your opponent.) [When Played] Play up to one [One For All] affinity card from your hand or sideline set to resting onto your energy line.
  • 2x Izuku Midoriya UE25BT/MHA-P-001 Energy 1 Buy
    If you have a [Class A] affinity card other than <Izuku Midoriya> on your field, this card cannot be played from your hand. [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) [During Your Turn] For each [One For All] affinity card with a unique card name on your field and in your sideline, this character gains 500 BP.

Events

  • 4x Faux 100% Manchester Smash UE25BT/MHA-2-034 Buy
    For each [One For All] affinity card with a unique card name on your field and in your sideline, reduce this card's required energy by [Yellow×1] while in your hand. Choose one character with {3000 or less BP} on your opponent's front line and sideline it. If <Izuku Midoriya> is on your field, {5000 or less BP} instead.
  • 4x Go Beyond UE25BT/MHA-2-035 Buy
    Choose up to two of your AP cards and switch them to active.

Sites

  • 4x En UE25BT/MHA-2-029 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Sideline This Card] You can only activate this ability if you have a combined total of three or more [One For All] affinity cards with unique card names on your field and in your sideline. Choose one <Izuku Midoriya> on your field. It gains "This character cannot be chosen by abilities on your opponent's characters, and it cannot be targeted by [Snipe] " until the start of your next turn. Draw a card.
  • 4x Hikage Shinomori UE25BT/MHA-2-030 Buy
    [During Your Turn] If you have a combined total of three or more [One For All] affinity cards with unique card names on your field and in your sideline and an <Izuku Midoriya> would leave your field due to one of your opponent's trigger abilities, you may sideline this site instead. If you do, switch that <Izuku Midoriya> to resting.
  • 4x Nana Shimura UE25BT/MHA-2-031 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Sideline This Card] You can only activate this ability if you have a combined total of three or more [One For All] affinity cards with unique card names on your field and in your sideline. Choose one <Izuku Midoriya> on your field and switch it to active.
  • 4x Daigoro Banjo UE25BT/MHA-2-032 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Place 1 Card From Hand Into Sideline] [Sideline This Card] You can only activate this ability if you have a combined total of three or more [One For All] affinity cards with unique card names on your field and in your sideline. Choose one <Izuku Midoriya> on your field. It gains [Snipe] (You may target a character on your opponent's front line and attack it with this character. If you do, your opponent cannot block) until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Third One For All Wielder UE25BT/MHA-2-033 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Sideline This Card] You can only activate this ability if you have a combined total of three or more [One For All] affinity cards with unique card names on your field and in your sideline. Choose one <Izuku Midoriya> on your field. It gains 2000 BP until the end of the turn. Draw a card.
