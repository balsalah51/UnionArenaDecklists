Characters

  • 4x Shunsui Kyoraku UE01NC/BLC-3-007 Energy 2 Buy
    [Draw] Draw a card.
  • 4x Sosuke Aizen UEX08BT/BLC-4-060 Energy 3 Buy
    This card cannot be played onto your front line and can only be moved to your front line by one of your abilities. [When Played] Choose up to one character on your field other than <Sosuke Aizen> and move it to the other line. [Activate: Main] [#If on the Energy Line#] Switch two active characters on your front line to resting. If you do, move this character to your front line. [When Attacking] Draw a card.
  • 4x Nanao Ise UEX08BT/BLC-4-064 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top three cards of your deck. Reveal up to one green event or site card and add it to your hand. Place the remaining cards on the bottom of your deck in any order. If you added a card to your hand, place one card from your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x Nanao Ise UEX08BT/BLC-4-065 Energy 3 Buy
    [Step] (During your movement phase, you may move this card from your front line to your energy line.) [During Your Turn] If <Shunsui Kyoraku> is on your energy line, this character gains 1500 BP. [Activate: Main] You can only activate this ability if you have used a <Shinken Hakkyoken> card this turn. Switch this character to active. You can only activate this <Nanao Ise> ability one time each turn.
  • 4x Retsu Unohana UEX08BT/BLC-4-066 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] You may place one card from your hand into your sideline. If you do, look at the top card of your deck, then place it on the top or bottom of your deck. Draw a card.
  • 4x Shunsui Kyoraku UEX08BT/BLC-4-067 Energy 1 Buy
    [When Played] Look at the top three cards of your deck. Place up to one card into your sideline, then place the remaining cards on the top of your deck in any order.
  • 4x Shunsui Kyoraku UEX08BT/BLC-4-069 Energy 5 Buy
    [Raid] <Shunsui Kyoraku> Switch to active. May move to the front line. At the end of this character's attack, if you have a total of four or more green event and site cards with unique card names in your sideline, choose up to one character on your energy line and swap it with this character. [When Played] Reduce the AP cost of the next [Katenkyokotsu] affinity card you use from your hand this turn by 1.
  • 4x Soi Fon UEX08BT/BLC-4-071 Energy 4 Buy
    [Step] (During your movement phase, you may move this card from your front line to your energy line.) [When Played] Draw a card, then place one card from your hand into your sideline. [Activate: Main] Switch this character to active. You can only activate this <Soi Fon> ability one time each turn.

Events

  • 4x Kurohitsugi UEX08BT/BLC-4-078 Buy
    If <Sosuke Aizen> is not on your field, this card's card name changes to <The Oozing Crest of Corruption! The Arrogant Vessel of Madness! Deny the Seething Urge, Stun and Flicker, Disrupt the Sleep! The Crawling Queen of Iron! The Eternally Self-Destructing Doll of Mud! Unite! Repulse! Fill the Earth, and Know Your Own Powerlessness!! Hado Number 90: Kurohitsugi!> in all locations. Choose one character with 4000 or less BP on your opponent's front line and sideline it.
  • 4x Bankai UEX08BT/BLC-4-080 Buy
    Choose up to two of your AP cards and switch them to active.
  • 4x Katenkyokotsu Karamatsu Shinju UEX08BT/BLC-4-081 Buy
    You can only use this card if <Shunsui Kyoraku> is on your field. Choose one character with 5000 or less BP on your opponent's front line and sideline it. Choose up to one <Katen> or <Kyokotsu> on your energy line and switch it to active.

Sites

  • 4x Katen UEX08BT/BLC-4-076 Buy
    [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Sideline This Card] Play up to one green <Shunsui Kyoraku> with 2 or less required energy from your sideline set to active onto your energy line.
  • 2x Kyokotsu UEX08BT/BLC-4-077 Buy
    [When Played] Add up to one <Shinken Hakkyoken> card from your sideline to your hand. [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Sideline This Card] Choose up to one card on your field and switch it to active. Choose up to one character on your field. It gains 1000 BP until the end of the turn.
