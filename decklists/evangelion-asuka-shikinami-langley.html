Evangelion - Asuka Shikinami Langley is an aggro list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep four-copy redundancy on Asuka Shikinami Langley (012)/(013) and Mari Makinami Illustrious (017)/(018) ensures the deck reliably opens with its core aggressive characters.. The inclusion of both 'A Hot Bath Lets You Wash Your Life and Begin Anew' and 'What Kind of Idiot Are You?' at 4 copies each gives consistent access to situational event effects that smooth draws or punish opponents..
