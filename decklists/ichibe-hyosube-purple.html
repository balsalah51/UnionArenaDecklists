    [When Played] You may place one other character from your field into your removal area. They gain 2000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 4x Ichibe Hyosube UEX08BT/BLC-4-056 Energy 4 Buy

[When Sidelined] Place this card into your removal area. Play up to one purple <Ichigo Kurosaki>, <The Royal Palace>, or [Squad Zero] affinity card with {0} or less required energy and 1 AP cost from your hand set to resting onto your field. For each [Squad Zero] affinity card with a unique card name in your removal area, add 1 to the required energy range of the character you may play with this ability.
