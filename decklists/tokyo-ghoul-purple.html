Tokyo Ghoul - Shu Tsukiyama

Tokyo Ghoul - Shu Tsukiyama is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across Ken Kaneki (040/041) and Shu Tsukiyama (049/050/052) variants gives the deck resilience to disruption and consistent access to its core threats turn after turn..
