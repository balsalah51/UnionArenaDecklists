How it plays

From public tournament lists

Sword Art Online - Sinon is an aggro-tempo list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep Sinon redundancy across six distinct card numbers (072, 073, 090, 091, 088, 066) means the deck reliably opens with a sniper-style attacker on curve.. Ultima Ratio Hecate II functions as a signature high-impact weapon/finisher that can swing a stalled board state in the deck's favor..
