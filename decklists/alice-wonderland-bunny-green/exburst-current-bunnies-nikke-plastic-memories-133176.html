    This card is also treated as <Alice>. [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) [When Played] Play up to one green [Tetra Line] affinity card with {0} or less required energy and 1 AP cost from your sideline set to active onto your field, or perform Raid with it. For each face-down card under a card on your field, add 1 to the required energy range of the character you may play or perform Raid with with this ability.
  • 4x Noir UP01BT/NIK-2-041 Energy 2 Buy
    If you have no cards on your field, reduce this card's required energy by [Green×2] while in your hand. [When Played] Draw a card, then place one card from your hand face down under a [Tetra Line] affinity card on your field.
  • 3x Rouge UP01BT/NIK-2-043 Energy 2 Buy
    If this character has a face-down card under it, it gains [Green] energy generation. [Activate: Main] [Switch to Resting] [Once Per Turn] Place the top card of your deck face down under this character.
  • 4x Soda: Twinkling Bunny UP01BT/NIK-2-044 Energy 2 Buy
    This card is also treated as <Soda>. [When Played] You may move any number of face-down cards under cards on your field under this character. If you move two or more cards, switch this character to active. You can only activate this <Soda: Twinkling Bunny> ability one time each turn. [When Attacking] You may place any number of face-down cards under this character into your sideline. If you do, for each card placed into your sideline with this ability, this character gains 500 BP until the end of the turn.
