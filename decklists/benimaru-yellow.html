How it plays

From public tournament lists

Benimaru (Yellow) is a midrange-tempo list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on its two namesake threats - four Shuna variants (006, 007, 017, 019) and four Benimaru numbers (024, 027, 011, 010) - gives the deck reliable access to its core game plan across multiple draws.. Hell Flare at 4 copies functions as a repeatable board-impact event that can swing tempo back in the deck's favor mid-game..
