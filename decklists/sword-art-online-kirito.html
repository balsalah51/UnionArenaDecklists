Sword Art Online - Kirito is an aggro-tempo list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep raid chains on both Kirito (071/084/086/087) and Sinon (072/073/088/090/091) allow the deck to snowball stats turn after turn once a base unit lands.. Ultima Ratio Hecate II gives the deck a recognizable high-impact payoff tied to Sinon's sniper identity, offering reach that generic aggro shells lack..
