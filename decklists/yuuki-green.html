How it plays

From public tournament lists

Yuuki (Green) is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across five distinct Asuna printings (068, 069, 071, 063, 070) means the deck reliably finds its namesake character across many hands and mulligans.. Yuuki support package (069, 078, 080, 068, 081) gives a secondary engine that can keep board presence steady even when Asuna draws are slow..
