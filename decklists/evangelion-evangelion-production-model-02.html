Evangelion - Evangelion Production Model-02

Evangelion - Evangelion Production Model-02 is a tempo list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The Model-02 into Beast Form raid chain (4x/4x) creates a reliable stat upgrade path that can outclass unraided boards in the mid-game.. Triple Asuka Shikinami Langley variants (086/088/087/106) give multiple angles of pressure and let the deck adapt its front-line composition to matchup needs..
