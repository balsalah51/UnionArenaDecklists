How it plays

From public tournament lists

Bleach - Rukia Kuchiki is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Sixteen copies of the Rukia Kuchiki versions (031, 034, 039, plus the base card) give a very high chance of cheap early energy-line bodies and Raid material.. The Renji Abarai (025/026/028) and Byakuya Kuchiki (029/030) lines give several Raid steps, so one board can grow into larger threats without much new energy..
