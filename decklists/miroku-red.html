How it plays

From public tournament lists

Miroku (Red) is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across four separate Sango numbers (056/057/058/059) and four Miroku numbers (068/069/070/071) means the deck rarely misses its core energy-line pieces even through mulligans.. Determine How to Live at 4 copies likely provides consistent card selection/filtering, smoothing out draws toward Shippo and key support pieces..
