How it plays

From public tournament lists

Naraku (Purple) is a midrange-control list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on key pieces - 4x Kagewaki Hitomi, 4x Kanna (021) and (022), and full playsets of Naraku (026-028) - means the deck rarely misses its core engine even through mulligans.. Multiple named Naraku variants let the deck layer disruption and board control over several turns, grinding down opponents who rely on a single key threat..
