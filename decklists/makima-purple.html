How it plays

From public tournament lists

Makima (Purple) is a midrange-control list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. The seven distinct Makima printings (018-024) give deep redundancy, meaning the deck rarely whiffs on finding a Makima piece to build around.. Denji support across three different numbers (004, 007, 008) provides a secondary aggressive or utility axis that keeps the opponent honest even if Makima is slow to assemble..
