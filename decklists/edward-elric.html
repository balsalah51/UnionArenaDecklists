How it plays

From public tournament lists

Fullmetal Alchemist - Edward Elric is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across both Elric brothers' numbered variants (4x Edward Elric 009/010/012, 4x Alphonse Elric 002/004) means the deck rarely whiffs on finding its core characters.. Equivalent Exchange and Gateway of Truth appear to function as filtering/search tools, smoothing draws toward the specific Elric pieces the deck needs turn to turn..
