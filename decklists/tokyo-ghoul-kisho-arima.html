How it plays

From public tournament lists

Tokyo Ghoul - Kisho Arima is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Strong raid-target redundancy (Arima/Suzuya at 4x each) gives above-average consistency (60) for a Tier4 deck. Deep redundancy on both Kisho Arima (002/003/004/base) and Juzo Suzuya (007-010) numbers means raid targets are almost always available, smoothing out the deck's core engine..
