    [Nullify Impact] (The character battling this character loses Impact for the duration of this battle.) This character must block your opponent's attacks if able.

[Activate: Main] Switch this character to active. You can only activate this <Soi Fon> ability one time each turn.

Choose one other [Thirteen Court Guard Squads] affinity card on your field and switch it to active.
