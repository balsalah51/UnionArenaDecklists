Sakamoto Days - Shishiba

Sakamoto Days - Shishiba is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep four-of investment in both the Shishiba (052/053/055) and Osaragi (042/044/045/046) lines gives the deck a stable, redundant core that resists being drawn out of its game plan..
