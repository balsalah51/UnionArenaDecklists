Kagurabachi - Chihiro Rokuhira

Kagurabachi - Chihiro Rokuhira is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy in the Chihiro Rokuhira raid line (060, 058, 059, 055, Winner) means the deck rarely bricks on finding its core identity piece across a 50-card pool.. Kunishige Rokuhira and Enten Kuro provide early-game bodies that can transition into energy or support raid setup without wasting tempo..
