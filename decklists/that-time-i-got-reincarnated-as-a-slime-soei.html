That Time I Got Reincarnated As A Slime - Soei

That Time I Got Reincarnated As A Slime - Soei is a tempo list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Soei's retire/board-interaction effects across its (059), (060), (058) and (1-021) variants let this deck proactively dismantle key opposing threats rather than racing on raw stats alone.. The dense Rimuru support package (P-001, 107, 017, 053, 050, 018) provides consistent card filtering and selection, smoothing access to Soei pieces and Feast/Report engines..
