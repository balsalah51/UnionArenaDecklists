The 100 Girlfriends - Shizuka Yoshimoto

The 100 Girlfriends - Shizuka Yoshimoto is a combo list. Standard tier 5 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on the identity card - four separate Shizuka Yoshimoto printings (061, 065, 066, 067) give the deck multiple copies of its core payoff across different cost points and effects, smoothing draws toward the game plan..
