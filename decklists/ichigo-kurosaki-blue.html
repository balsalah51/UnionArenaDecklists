Ichigo Kurosaki (Blue) is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy across many Ichigo Kurosaki card numbers (016, 017, 018, 019, 039, 040, 041, 051, 103, 104) ensures the player almost always has a playable Ichigo on curve regardless of draw variance.. Bankai (066) offers a powerful mid-to-late game Raid or power spike that can swing a board state once enough Ichigo pieces are established on the front line..
