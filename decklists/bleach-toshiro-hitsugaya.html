Bleach - Toshiro Hitsugaya

Bleach - Toshiro Hitsugaya is a midrange list. Standard tier 4 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Toshiro Hitsugaya's raid line across three printings (083, 085, base) lets skilled pilots build toward a significantly upgraded threat mid-game that outclasses unraided bodies.. The deep Kenpachi Zaraki suite (066, 067, 080, 081, base) gives the deck multiple aggressive stat-line options to fill curve gaps when Toshiro pieces are drawn out of sequence..
