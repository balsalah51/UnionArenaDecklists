Kagurabachi - Red

Kagurabachi - Red is a aggro list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy in Chihiro Rokuhira (060/058/059/055) and Hakuri Sazanami (047/046/045/044) printings gives the deck reliable access to its core threats turn after turn.. Raid lines built around the repeated Chihiro Rokuhira and Hakuri Sazanami names let the deck upgrade board presence mid-combat without extra AP overhead once positioned correctly..
