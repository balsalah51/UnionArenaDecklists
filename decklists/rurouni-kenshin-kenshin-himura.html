Rurouni Kenshin - Kenshin Himura

Rurouni Kenshin - Kenshin Himura is a midrange list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Seven distinct Kenshin Himura printings (085, 106, 090, 087, 088, 089, 105) create a deep raid chain that lets the deck consistently find an upgrade target regardless of which copy is drawn.. Triple Kaoru Kamiya lines (071, 101, 068) at 4 copies each provide a stable secondary engine that can support the board while Kenshin scales up through raids..
