  • 4x Charlotte Chuhlhourne UEX01BT/BLC-2-039 Energy 2 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 3x Cirucci Sanderwicci UEX01BT/BLC-2-040 Energy 0 Buy
  • 3x Dordoni Alessandro Del Socaccio UEX01BT/BLC-2-041 Energy 1 Buy
    [Active] Choose one character on your field and switch it to active. It gains 3000 BP until the end of the turn.
  • 2x Luppi Antenor UEX01BT/BLC-2-042 Energy 0 Buy
    [Draw] Draw a card.
  • 1x Orihime Inoue UEX08BT/BLC-4-022 Energy 4 Buy
    Play this character set to active. [When Played] You may place one card from your hand into your sideline. If you do, add up to one blue character card with 1 or less required energy from your sideline to your hand, or play it set to resting onto your field. [Activate: Main] [Once Per Turn] You can only activate this ability on the turn this character is played. Choose one character on your field. It gains "This character cannot be chosen by your opponent's abilities" until the start of your next turn.
  • 4x Yoruichi Shihoin UEX08BT/BLC-4-030 Energy 0 Buy
    [When Played] You may place one card from your hand into your sideline. If you do, look at the top card of your deck, place it on the top or bottom of your deck, then draw a card.
  • 2x Nemu Kurotsuchi UEX08BT/BLC-4-040 Energy 2 Buy
    [When Played] Draw a card, then place one card from your hand into your sideline.
  • 4x Nemu Kurotsuchi UEX08BT/BLC-4-041 Energy 4 Buy
    At the start of your turn, sideline this character. [When Played] If <Mayuri Kurotsuchi> is on your front line, you may sideline one other character on your field. If you do, switch this character to active and give it "Your opponent must block this character's attacks if able" and [Impact (1)] until the end of the turn. [When Sidelined] Place this card into your removal area.
  • 4x Mayuri Kurotsuchi UEX08BT/BLC-4-042 Energy 3 Buy
    [When Played] Choose one of the following: ・Look at the top two cards of your deck. Place any number of them on the top of your deck in any order, then place any remaining cards into your sideline. ・You may place two cards from your hand into your sideline. If you do, add up to one <Mayuri Kurotsuchi> or <Nemu Kurotsuchi> card from your sideline to your hand. ・You may sideline one other character on your field. If you do, play up to one blue <Kurotsuchi Corpse Unit> from your hand set to active onto your energy line.
