Evangelion - Purple

Evangelion - Purple is a control list. Standard tier 2 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy on Shinji Ikari (027/029/031) and Misato Katsuragi (044/045/046) gives the deck multiple copies of its core engine pieces, smoothing draws across long games.. Mari Makinami Illustrious (054/056) doubled inclusion suggests strong synergy payoffs when both variants are in play, likely enabling combo-style board states..
