How it plays

From public tournament lists

Emilia (Yellow) is a midrange-tempo list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep Emilia redundancy (022, 023, 025, 026, 027, 105, 106) means the deck rarely misses its core character on the energy or front line, smoothing early development.. Puck (001) and Puck (002) provide cheap, repeatable support pieces that help stabilize the energy line in the opening turns..
