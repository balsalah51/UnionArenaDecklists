How it plays

From public tournament lists

Emilia (Yellow) is a midrange-tempo list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Eight Pucks (001/002) plus Spiritual Arts User give reliable 0-2 energy bodies, so the energy line is built early and the AP is spent efficiently on turns 1-3.. Heavy playsets of the Emilia numbers (022, 023, 025, 026, 027, 106) make the opening hand and Raid targets predictable..
