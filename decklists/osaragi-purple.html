How it plays

From public tournament lists

Osaragi (Purple) is a midrange list. Standard tier 5 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Four copies each of three Osaragi cards give it a clear body to deploy and a reasonable Osaragi chance in the opening hands.. Taro Sakamoto variants (047/049/050/051) fill the curve with mid-cost characters that can trade on the front line..
