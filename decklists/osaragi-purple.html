How it plays

From public tournament lists

Osaragi (Purple) is a midrange list. Standard tier 5 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Deep redundancy with four copies each of multiple Osaragi, Taro Sakamoto, and Nagumo numbers means the deck rarely whiffs on its core characters across a 50-card build.. The character-heavy, low-event count suggests a stable energy line can be assembled turn after turn without relying on fragile combo pieces..
