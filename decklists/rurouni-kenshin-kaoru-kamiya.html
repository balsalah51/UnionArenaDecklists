Rurouni Kenshin - Kaoru Kamiya

Rurouni Kenshin - Kaoru Kamiya is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Rurouni Kenshin - Kaoru Kamiya is a midrange list. Standard tier 3 on the latest TCG Contender snapshot. Extremely deep character redundancy - with essentially every printing of Kaoru Kamiya and Kenshin Himura run at or near 4 copies, the deck rarely whiffs on finding its core pieces.. Oath Not to Kill and Ryuutsuisen at full playset give the deck consistent access to key event effects that support the Kenshin Himura game plan turn after turn..
