Standard format

Lists on this site are 50-card constructed Union Arena decks. English events are single-title Standard. A deck is usually one IP (Solo Leveling, Sakamoto Days, Evangelion, Chainsaw Man) plus up to 4 copies of each card number. Browse every title on the titles index.

Current metagame

From lists on this site

The snapshot follows public Union Arena tournaments. Sung Jinwoo, Hajime Saito, Shin Asakura, and Rei Ayanami are the names that keep showing up. Everything else is a step down or a title specialist.

  • Solo Leveling - Sung Jinwoo Midrange · Tier 1

    The four-tier Sung Jinwoo raid chain (018→022→019→017, capped by Sung Jinwoo Winner) gives the deck a scaling threat that gets harder to answer each turn it survives.

  • Evangelion - Evangelion Test Type-01 Pseudo DMS Phase Tempo · Tier 1

    Strong early curve with 4x Shinji Ikari (074) and 4x Shinji Ikari (075) enabling proactive board development from turn one.

  • Rurouni Kenshin - Kaoru Kamiya Midrange · Tier 3

    Extremely deep character redundancy - with essentially every printing of Kaoru Kamiya and Kenshin Himura run at or near 4 copies, the deck rarely whiffs on finding its core pieces.

  • Evangelion - Rei Ayanami Tempo · Tier 1

    Highest consistency score among Tier 1 decks via four Rei printings

  • Chainsaw Man - Denji Aggro · Tier 2

    Highest disruption (52) of the pure CSM aggro sub-archetypes via Bomb Devil's burst effects

  • Chainsaw Man Aggro · Tier 2

    Rising meta share (4.4%) among the fastest-growing decks in the format

  • Sakamoto Days - Shin Asakura Aggro · Tier 1

    Best matchup spread among Tier 1 aggro decks (63) thanks to strong performance vs control

  • Sakamoto Days - Heisuke Mashimo Tempo · Tier 2

    Rising meta share (3.7%) reflects real tournament traction despite modest raw stats

What is Union Arena Standard format?

English events are single-title Standard constructed. A deck is usually one anime or manga IP plus up to 4 copies of each card number.
